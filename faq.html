Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on delivery, allergens and custom orders.

How long does delivery take?

Local orders are delivered within 1 to 3 working days. You can also collect from our kitchen during trading hours.

Do your cookies contain allergens?

Yes. Our cookies contain wheat, eggs, milk and may contain nuts. Please contact us before ordering if you have an allergy.

Can I order a custom gift box?

Yes. Use the contact form to tell us the occasion, quantity and flavours, and we will send you a quote.

Do you offer wholesale orders?

Yes. We supply cafés and gift shops. Send an enquiry with your expected weekly quantity.

How should I store my cookies?

Keep them in an airtight container at room temperature and enjoy within five days.
